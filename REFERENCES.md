### Market methodology

- analyse the market as a whole, not dealer-vs-private segments;
- deduplicate the same car across platforms;
- remove wrecks, projects, cars not running and non-representative order-only listings;
- reject artificial credit/trade-in bait prices, or use clearly stated cash price;
- treat displayed values as asking prices, not completed-transaction prices;
- rare Turbo/GTS/Turbo Performance ranges have lower statistical stability.
